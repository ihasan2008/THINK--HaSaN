## 📖 About

This repository is a personal space for collecting knowledge, exploring ideas, researching different subjects, and developing a deeper understanding of the world.

It covers a wide range of topics — from science and technology to humanity, religion, philosophy, society, and the environment.

The goal is to explore different perspectives, ask meaningful questions, think critically, and continuously expand knowledge.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Explore different fields of knowledge.
- Understand the world and how it works.
- Develop logical and critical thinking.
- Research different ideas and perspectives.
- Ask questions and seek evidence-based answers.
- Understand humans, society, nature, and systems.
- Build a strong foundation of general and specialized knowledge.

## 💡 Core Principles

- **Curiosity** — Always ask questions.
- **Learning** — Never stop acquiring knowledge.
- **Thinking** — Analyze before accepting ideas.
- **Research** — Seek reliable information and evidence.
- **Understanding** — Focus on understanding, not memorization.
- **Open-mindedness** — Consider different perspectives.
- **Growth** — Continuously improve knowledge and thinking.

## 🔍 Learning Approach

1. **Explore** — Discover new topics and ideas.
2. **Question** — Identify what needs to be understood.
3. **Research** — Gather information from reliable sources.
4. **Analyze** — Examine facts, evidence, and perspectives.
5. **Understand** — Develop a clear understanding.
6. **Document** — Organize and preserve knowledge.
7. **Reflect** — Reconsider ideas and improve understanding.
